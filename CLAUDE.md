## Working with the owner
- The owner is not a developer. Explain every change in plain English, end each task with a
  checklist they can follow on the Vercel preview link, and say which changes need developer review
  (anything touching payments, personal data or the draw engine).
- Build one phase task at a time (AGENTS.md section 10). Plan before building.
- Sandbox/test keys only. Never ask for live keys, passwords or real card numbers in chat; ask the
  owner to add test values to the environment settings instead.
- Legal wording (terms, free entry route, age/odds statements, share captions) is supplied by the
  solicitor. Use clearly marked placeholders until it arrives.
